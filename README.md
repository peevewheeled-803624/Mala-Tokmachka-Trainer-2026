The Legend of Mala Tokmachka is a 2D tower defense and fortress-building strategy game developed and published by The Current Thing. It was released for PC on August 15, 2026. Players build a vertical fortress block by block and defend it against escalating waves of ground and air attacks.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Legend of Mala Tokmachka**. This trainer provides control over the resources and defensive systems used to construct, repair, upgrade, and operate the fortress. It is designed for the single-player PC version and supports siege runs focused on experimentation, rapid construction, and extended survival.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to experiment with fortress layouts, test weapons and support systems, explore later siege stages, reduce resource management, or create relaxed single-player challenge runs.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Money** — Prevents the available money pool from decreasing while constructing defenses, repairing structures, expanding the base, or upgrading weapons.
- **Unlimited Experience** — Keeps experience available for progression and upgrades throughout the current siege.
- **Free Construction and Upgrades** — Removes the resource cost of placing fortress blocks, weapons, support modules, power systems, radar equipment, and defensive platforms.
- **Instant Repairs** — Completes fortress repairs immediately and prevents repair actions from consuming normal resources.
- **Unlimited Power** — Maintains the power required by the fortress infrastructure and defensive modules.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Invulnerable Fortress** — Prevents defensive blocks and installed systems from losing structural integrity when attacked.
- **Maximum Weapon Damage** — Boosts the damage output of installed weapons against ground units, aircraft, armored vehicles, drones, missiles, and other attacking forces.
- **Unlimited Weapon Operation** — Keeps active defensive weapons operational without normal resource or operating limitations.
- **Freeze Siege Pressure** — Stabilizes the current attack state so players can reorganize the fortress and test defensive layouts without normal escalation.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game does not use a roaming player character. Navigation focuses on viewing the vertical fortress and selecting locations for new blocks and defensive systems. Pause the game before making major structural changes when using the trainer.

### Tools & Inventory

The main resource systems are money, experience, repairs, expansion capacity, and power. Activate the relevant trainer options before placing blocks, installing weapons, repairing damaged sections, or upgrading key systems.

### World & Environment

The battlefield is a nameless frontier village under an endless siege. Build upward or outward using walls, weapons, support modules, power systems, radar equipment, repair blocks, and defensive platforms. Enemy waves grow more dangerous over time and combine ground and air threats.

### Combat & Weapons

Use overlapping fire zones and match defensive systems to the incoming threat. Machine guns and cannons handle conventional targets, anti-air systems counter aircraft and drones, while lasers and missile platforms provide specialized coverage. Activate fortress protection before a heavy wave and use maximum weapon damage to test the full defensive arsenal.
